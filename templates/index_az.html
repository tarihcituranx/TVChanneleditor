Welcome to TV Channel Editor! 👋

No more struggling with your TV's remote control for hours! Just export your channel list file to a USB drive and upload it here. Easily organize your channels with drag-and-drop or use the "Magic Wand" 🪄 to sort them in seconds.

Your file is only processed temporarily on our server to read the channel list. It is never permanently archived.
