🛡️ Privacy Policy (KVKK and GDPR Information Notice)

In short: We operate under the “Privacy-First” principle. You do not provide us with your name, email address, or any personal information. The TV channel files you upload to the system are stored only in the server’s temporary (temp) directory for processing and are permanently deleted upon completion (within 1 hour at the latest). We do not sell, track, or archive any of your data.

1. What Data Do We Collect?

There is no registration, membership, or login system. Therefore, we do not collect any directly identifiable personal data such as first name, last name, email, phone number, or similar information.

We only collect the TV Channel List (.scm, .zip) files that you intentionally upload to the system as a technical necessity during the editing process.

2. Retention Period and Deletion of Uploaded Files

The channel files you upload to the system are stored in a temporary folder so that a download link can be generated.
  • Edited Files: Once the download is successfully completed or exactly 60 minutes (1 hour) after the page loads, our system automatically and permanently deletes them.
  • Cross-Device Transfer (Via Code): The draft cache created when you use the “Transfer to Device” button self-deletes after 10 minutes.
None of your channel lists are archived, backed up, or stored on our servers.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

We absolutely do not use cookies for advertising, behavioral analytics, or user tracking on our site. To prevent your work from being lost in the event of potential internet or power outages while you’re editing your channels, data is automatically saved as a draft in your browser’s local storage—not on our servers. Once you’ve successfully downloaded your file, this draft is automatically deleted from your browser.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

Our system is not affiliated with any television manufacturers (Samsung, LG, Sony, etc.) or marketing companies. The data you upload or your usage statistics cannot be sold, shared, or used for marketing purposes by third parties.

5. Your Rights Under the KVKK (Turkey) and GDPR (European Union)

The system is designed with data minimization and temporary processing principles in mind. Since personal data (including IP logs) is not permanently stored, we do not maintain a database that would require you to exercise your right to erasure or the right to be forgotten; the system completely “forgets” you the moment your visit ends.

6. Data Controller and Contact Information

The system is an independent, volunteer-driven open-source developer initiative. For any questions regarding the KVKK or technical processes, or for data requests, you may contact the Data Controller, Turan Kaya. To reach us, you can open an “Issue” on our official GitHub page or send an email directly to tarihcituranx@proton.me.
