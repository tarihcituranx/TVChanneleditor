✅ Full Support
Samsung Smart TV
All devices from the older generation (E, F, H) and the newer Tizen generation (J, K, M, Q, R, T) series are supported. Sorting and renaming are fully compatible.
.scm .zip (SQLite)
✅ Partial Support
LG Smart TV
Our system supports LG's new generation XML-based .tll (GlobalClone) files. Older binary .tll files are not supported; for those files, please use the ChanSort desktop application.
.tll (XML)
✅ Full Support
Sony BRAVIA
Sony BRAVIA’s XML-based channel list format is readable and writable. Includes new 2024+ models. Channel name and reordering are supported.
sdb.xml
✅ Full Support
Hisense Smart TV
The SQLite-based service list format for the 2017 and 2021 generations is supported. Channel sorting, renaming, and hiding are supported.
servicelist.db
