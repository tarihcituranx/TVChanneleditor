🛡️ Privacy Policy (GDPR / KVKK)

In short: We operate on a "Privacy-First" principle. You do not provide your name, email, or any personal information. The TV channel files you upload are only temporarily held in the server's memory to process your edits and are permanently deleted within 1 hour. We do not sell, track, or store your data.

1. What Data Do We Collect?

There is no registration, membership, or login system. Therefore, we do not collect any directly identifiable personal data such as Name, Email, or Phone number.

We only process the Television Channel List (.scm, .zip) files you upload out of technical necessity to perform the editing operations you request.

2. File Retention and Deletion

The channel files you upload are hosted in a temporary folder so a download link can be generated.
  • Edited Files: Are permanently and automatically destroyed by our system as soon as the download is successful or exactly 60 minutes (1 Hour) after page load.
  • Cross-Device Drafts: Temporary drafts created via the "Export to Device" code automatically self-destruct after 10 minutes.
None of your channel lists are archived, backed up, or stored on our servers.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

We strictly do not use advertising (Ads), behavioral (Analytics), or user tracking (Tracking) cookies on our site. To prevent your hard work from being lost in case of internet or power outages while editing, your data is saved automatically as a draft only inside your computer's browser (Local Storage) without being sent to the server. Once you successfully download your file, this draft is automatically deleted from your browser.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

Our system is not affiliated with any television manufacturer (Samsung, LG, Sony, etc.) or marketing company. The data you upload or your usage statistics cannot be sold, shared, or used for marketing purposes by third parties.

5. Your GDPR and KVKK Rights

Our system fully complies with "Privacy by Design" principles. Because we do not persistently store personal data (including IP logs), there is no database for you to exercise a "right to be forgotten" or deletion request; the system inherently "forgets" you the moment your session ends.

6. Data Controller and Contact

This system is a volunteer open-source independent developer initiative. For any questions regarding privacy or data requests, you can directly contact the Data Controller by opening an "Issue" on the project's official GitHub page.
