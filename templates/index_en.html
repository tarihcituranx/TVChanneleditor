Welcome to TV Channel Editor! 👋

No more struggling with your TV's remote control for hours! Just export your channel list file to a USB drive and upload it here. Easily organize your channels with drag-and-drop or use the "Smart Templates" 🪄 to sort them in seconds.

Your file is only processed temporarily on our server to read the channel list. It is never permanently archived.

Drop your channel list here

.scm · .zip · sdb.xml · servicelist.db

Secure Operation: Your files are only processed during editing and are not stored.

🔒 No Signup  |  🚫 No Ads  |  ⚡ Processed Temporarily, Not Kept  |  📖 Open Source

⚡ Quick Start in 3 Steps

1

Export to USB

Export the channel list from your TV menu (Broadcasting > Expert Settings) to a USB drive (FAT32).

2

Upload Here

Drag and drop the file (.scm, .tll, etc.) from the USB into the area above. The format is auto-detected.

3

Edit and Download

Move, delete, or sort channels. When done, download it and import it back to your TV.
