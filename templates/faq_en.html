❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What Is an SCM File?
SCM (Samsung Channel Map) is an encrypted (checksum) data file that stores the satellite frequencies and channel lists for Samsung TVs. It is obtained by transferring it from your TV to a USB drive.
How do I get the SCM file from my TV?
Go to the menu on your TV: Broadcast > Channel Settings > Transfer Channel List > Transfer to USB. This process will save an .scm file to your USB drive.
How do I load the edited list onto the TV?
Copy the new SCM file you downloaded from this site to your USB flash drive. Then, you can upload it by selecting Broadcast > Channel Settings > Transfer Channel List > Import from USB in the TV menu.
How does Magic Wand match channels?
Since frequencies can change, the Magic Wand algorithm performs the match based on the Channel Name. It updates only the order of the channel named "TRT1 HD" in your original file while preserving all encrypted PIDs and frequency data.
Are other TV brands supported?
Currently, our system is designed specifically to crack the Checksum algorithm used by Samsung TVs. Other brands may be added in the future.
